Critical Reception

  • "An artist who refuses to be indexed." (Independent curator, year unknown)
  • "His very existence is a work. The absence of an archive is part of the work." (Unsigned comment)
  • "You buy his painting, but you'll never know if it's actually him you bought. That might be his only honesty." (Anonymous online buyer)
  • "He reminds me of names that only appeared in underground publications — names that never entered art history, but have always been there." (Art writer, published in a now-defunct magazine)
  • "The Wang Yingru problem is this: when you refuse all systems of verification, how do you prove you did those things? His answer, presumably: I don't need to prove it." (Source unknown)
  • "He turned the dodge in boxing into an artistic strategy — you can never hit him, because he's never where you're aiming." (Performance art festival participant, year unknown)
